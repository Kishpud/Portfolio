🔍 Designing Digital Platforms for Post-Monarchy Societies

In the aftermath of monarchy transitions, many countries face a critical gap in trust, governance, and citizen engagement. Nepal, as a post-monarchy society, has a unique opportunity to reinvent how citizens interact with the state using technology.

This article explores how we can develop secure, inclusive digital platforms that help fill that trust vacuum—tools that enable transparency, promote grassroots participation, and reduce bureaucratic corruption through well-architected civic tech systems.

From decentralizing decision-making using blockchain to building digital identities that respect privacy and cultural diversity, we can create solutions that are not only effective but respectful to Nepal's heritage and aspirations.
