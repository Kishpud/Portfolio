🌐 Bridging Global Tools to Local Problems

Technology built in one part of the world can solve problems in another—if adapted with cultural and social understanding. Open-source tools, APIs, and scalable platforms offer Nepal and other developing nations a head start in innovation.

This article explores case studies where global technologies—like open data dashboards, blockchain identity, or AI health assistants—were adapted to address rural access, education gaps, and governance issues.

The challenge is not just building tools, but reshaping them so they serve local needs with dignity, accessibility, and long-term sustainability.
