🛡️ Cybersecurity as a Pillar of Democracy

In the digital era, a nation’s democratic strength is tied to its digital resilience. Secure digital infrastructure enables free expression, fair elections, and access to information—all vital components of a functioning democracy.

This article discusses the role of cybersecurity not just as a technical safeguard, but as a cornerstone of modern governance. From defending electoral processes to protecting activists and journalists, security ensures voices aren’t silenced and rights aren’t violated.

For countries like Nepal, investing in cybersecurity is an investment in stability, sovereignty, and sustainable democratic growth.
